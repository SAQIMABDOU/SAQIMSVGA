Welcome to SAQIM SVGA

Al-mowqiq al-iḥtirafi al-awwal al-motakhassif f incha3 w tatwir kol ma howa mota3alliq bi baramij d-dardacha s-sawtiya (Voice Chat Applications) bi aḥdat at-tiknolojiyat al-motabaṭṭira.

Voice Chat Specialization

Nataqaddam bi ḥoloul ra2ida f incha3 l-hadaya al-motaḥarrika (Animated Gifts), 3alamat al-tamayoz (VIP Badges), maqa'id s-sawt (Mic Positions), w ikطارat al-avatar al-mokhassasa li baramij d-dardacha s-sawtiya.

Advanced Technologies

Nastanid ila aḥdat at-tiknolojiyat w siyagh al-motaṭṭira (SVGA, VAP, MP4, PAG, JSON, WEBP, MOV) li daman jawda 3aliya w ada2 salis dakhil l-baramij.

Dynamic Dashboard Management

Noarifo lakom niẓam idara motaqaddim yumkinokom min raf3 al-mawarid w at-ta'dil 3alayha aw ḥadfiha bi shokl morin w sari3 mobashara men lawḥat al-qiyada.

Custom Requests & Tailored Resources

La naqif 3inda al-mawarid al-jaheza faḥasab; bal noqaddim imkaniyat incha3 ay mawrid taḥt ṭalab al-zaboun (Custom Requests) bi ḥasab raġbatiho al-khassa, ma3 imkaniyat raf3ha w idaratiha bi kol sohola.
